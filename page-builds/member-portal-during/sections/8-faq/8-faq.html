How do I join each session?
Use the green Join Your Forum button — it's at the top of this page and in the menu bar throughout the weekend. It opens your Zoom room directly.
When should I log in each day?
The room opens at 8:30 AM each morning, in the time zone of your Forum. Plan to arrive at least 15 minutes before the 9:00 AM start so you're settled when we begin.
What if I get disconnected?
Just click Join Your Forum again to come right back in. If you have ongoing trouble, reach our team using the contact details below.
Where do I find each day's assignment?
Right here on this page, in the “Your assignments” section. Each day's assignment appears the morning it opens, with the newest at the top.
Can I invite guests to graduation?
Yes — Tuesday Evening Graduation is open to the family, friends, and colleagues you'd love to have there. Use the Graduation section above to invite them.
What if I need to transfer my course?
No problem — plans change. Contact us at info@landmarkworldwide.com or +1 (800) 888-9590, and for a small $35 administrative fee we'll transfer you to another course date that works for you.
Who do I contact if I need help?
Our support team is here throughout. You'll find our email and phone in the Contact section just below.
